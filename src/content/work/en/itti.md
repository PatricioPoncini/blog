- Take part in product architecture decisions, using event-driven and CQRS patterns to separate read and write loads in high-traffic services.
- Model schemas and indexes in DynamoDB and DocumentDB based on specific access patterns.
- Observability for distributed systems with New Relic: dashboards, NRQL queries, service monitoring and alerts.
