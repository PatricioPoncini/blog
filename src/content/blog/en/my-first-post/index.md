## Welcome to my blog! 👋

After many years of learning, working and experimenting with technologies like Go, TypeScript, Vue, databases, microservices and more, I finally decided to create this space.

The idea of this blog is simple: **share what I learn**, what works for me (and what doesn't), write down ideas, problems I solved, tricks, good practices and anything that might help someone else… or my future self.

I will write about topics like:

- Architecture and design patterns
- Backend development
- Databases
- Testing, automation and good practices
- Personal thoughts on learning and working in tech

I will also document personal projects and experiments, and maybe share some day-to-day opinions as a developer.

Thanks for stopping by.  
**This is the start of something I hope will grow over time.**

See you in the next post 🚀
